Privacy Policy for Study and Notify

Effective date: 5 October 2026
App: Study and Notify (Android, package name com.cangkyzies.studynotify)
Developer: cangkyzies
Contact: cangkyzies@gmail.com

The short version

  • Everything you put into Study and Notify stays on your phone.
  • There is no account, no sign-in and no server. The developer cannot see your study material, reminders, tasks or ratings.
  • The app has no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it does not sell or share your information with anyone.
  • A file only leaves the app when you choose to export or back up, and only goes where you send it.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy explains how the Study and Notify app handles information. "We" and "the developer" mean the person who makes the app, cangkyzies. "You" means the person using the app.

2. What information the app stores

The app keeps the following on your device so it can work:

  • Your study material: the text you paste in, split into items, plus the groups you organise them in (group names, which groups are on or off).
  • Your review history: the ratings you give each item ("Got it", "Partly", "Forgot" or your own labels) and the weights the app uses to decide how often an item comes back.
  • Your reminders: their titles, notes, times, repeat days, end dates and whether they are pinned.
  • Your daily tasks: task names, the days they are due, which days you completed them, and your streaks.
  • Your settings: review interval, quiet hours, rating-button labels, theme and dark mode, and similar choices.

All of this is saved in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it, and it is never sent to the developer or to any server.

3. What the app does not do

Study and Notify does not:

  • ask you to create an account or sign in;
  • collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal details;
  • use advertising, an advertising ID, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services;
  • send your information over the internet for any feature;
  • sell, rent, trade or share your information with anyone.

4. Exporting, backing up and importing (only when you choose)

  • Export and backup. When you tap Export (for a group, your tasks, or a full backup), the app creates a file and opens Android's share menu. You then choose where it goes, for example a cloud drive, an email or a messaging app. After that, the file is handled by the service you picked and by that service's own privacy policy. The developer never receives a copy.
  • Import and restore. The app only reads a file when you pick it in Android's file picker. It reads that one file and nothing else on your phone.

Backup files are plain text (JSON). They are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere you trust.

5. Android's own device backup

Like most Android apps, Study and Notify allows Android's built-in backup. If you have turned on backup in your phone's settings, Android may include this app's data in your phone's backup to your own Google account. That backup is run by Google under your Google account settings, not by the developer, and the developer cannot access it. You can turn it off in your phone's settings (usually Settings > Google > Backup, depending on your phone).

6. Permissions the app asks for, and why

  • NotificationsPOST_NOTIFICATIONS
    To show your review items, reminders and the daily "tasks left" message. Android asks you before allowing this, and you can turn it off at any time.
  • Alarms & remindersSCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM
    So reminders can arrive at the exact clock time you set. You can switch this off in your phone's settings; reminders may then arrive a few minutes late.
  • Run at startupRECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED
    To set your notifications up again after your phone restarts.
  • Keep the phone awake briefly, and vibrateWAKE_LOCK VIBRATE
    To deliver notifications reliably and let them vibrate.
  • Foreground serviceFOREGROUND_SERVICE
    Added by Android's standard background-work library (WorkManager). The app uses that library for a short background job, roughly every 30 minutes, that books your upcoming notifications on the phone. It does not send anything anywhere.
  • Network accessINTERNET ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE
    Added automatically by software libraries the app is built with. None of the app's features use the internet, and the app does not send your information anywhere.

The app does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or your phone's general storage.

7. How long information is kept, and how to delete it

Your information stays on your phone until you delete it. You can:

  • delete single items, groups, reminders or tasks inside the app; or
  • delete everything by uninstalling the app, or by going to Settings > Apps > Study and Notify > Storage > Clear storage on your phone.

Because the developer never receives your information, there is nothing held by the developer to delete. Any backup files you exported yourself are yours to keep or delete.

8. Security

Your information is kept in the app's private storage, which Android protects from other apps. Nothing is sent over the internet by the app. Exported backup files are not encrypted, so store and share them with care.

9. Children

Study and Notify is not designed for or directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. Because the app collects no personal information at all, it does not collect any information from children either. If you are a parent or guardian with a question, please contact us at cangkyzies@gmail.com.
